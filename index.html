01 / Overview

About the project

May’s Corpus is a source-data and documentation project supporting May’s Codex, a digital study environment for biblical and Qur’anic texts and related resources.

It brings source identification, data organization, editorial history, and rights information together in one place.

The term Scripture is used as a descriptive category for texts regarded as sacred within their respective religious traditions. Its use does not assert a theological position about the origin, authorship, or status of any text.

The project is being developed privately for now. A future public release will depend on source verification, documentation, and the rights applicable to each dataset.

03 / Documentation

Sources, processing & verification

01

Source identification

Record the author or editor, work title, edition, publication details, source location, language, and applicable license or rights statement.

02

Data processing

Describe extraction, OCR, normalization, correction, editorial revision, translation, transliteration, and conversion into structured formats where applicable.

03

Review scope

State what was checked, how it was checked, and which limitations remain. Review does not guarantee that a dataset is error-free.

04

Version history

Track changes to datasets and metadata so future maintainers can understand what changed and, where possible, why.

Editorial principle

"Keep source material distinguishable from extracted, corrected, translated, transliterated, or revised data. Where practical, preserve an unchanged source copy or raw extraction separately from edited versions."

04 / Contribution labels

Editorial contribution record

These labels describe the type of work performed on a dataset. They do not, by themselves, establish ownership, accuracy, endorsement, or permission to redistribute the underlying source.

EXBN

Extracted by Najma

Data extracted or transcribed by Najma from an identified source into the project’s working format.

EDBN

Edited by Najma

Data reviewed or edited by Najma for purposes such as OCR correction, consistency, formatting, or readability, while aiming to preserve the source’s meaning.

TRBN

Translated and/or Transliterated by Najma

Text translated between languages and/or transliterated between writing systems by Najma. The specific operation should be stated in the dataset metadata.

Multiple labels may apply to the same dataset. Assign labels only where the described contribution was actually performed.

05 / Responsible use

Rights & licensing

Rights and redistribution conditions must be assessed for each source and edition. A work’s age, its availability online, or the fact that it has been extracted or edited does not by itself establish that every version or component may be redistributed.

Metadata should identify the relevant source, any known license or public-domain information, and unresolved questions. Where rights have not yet been confirmed, that uncertainty should be stated rather than inferred.

Until the review is complete, the repository and its datasets remain private. Any future public release will be considered on a collection-by-collection basis.

Long-term preservation

Document the source. Preserve the work.
