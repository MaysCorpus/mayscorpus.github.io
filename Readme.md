# May’s Corpus

**A collection of structured textual corpora, source texts, and scholarly resources for May’s Codex.**

## Overview

May’s Corpus is a collection of structured textual data developed to support May’s Codex, a project dedicated to studying biblical, Qur’anic, and related textual traditions through their original languages and associated scholarly resources.

The repository contains textual corpora, word-level data, and commentaries prepared from identifiable source materials. Depending on the dataset, the data may have been extracted, edited, translated, transliterated, or otherwise processed by Najma.

The project aims to preserve source traceability, document editorial contributions, and provide a structured foundation that can be maintained and further developed in the future.

## Editorial Contributions

Some datasets are derived from source texts and undergo additional processing by Najma. Such work may involve text extraction, OCR correction, textual verification, editorial revision, translation, or transliteration.

For commentaries extracted from digital books or publications, the process may include correcting OCR errors, restoring incomplete or corrupted text where sufficiently supported by the source, and revising difficult sentence structures to improve readability for contemporary readers.

These revisions aim to preserve the author's original arguments, interpretations, qualifications, and intended meaning. Where applicable, the resulting text is also manually checked against the available source material and its corresponding biblical or Qur’anic passages.

Digital tools, including OCR software and AI-assisted processing, may be used as part of the workflow. Such tools support the editorial process but do not replace manual review and source verification.

The nature and extent of these contributions vary by dataset and are documented wherever applicable.

## Contribution Labels

The following labels identify the types of work performed on individual datasets:

- **EXBN — Extracted by Najma:** Text extracted independently from an identifiable source, rather than adopted from an existing structured corpus.
- **EDBN — Edited by Najma:** Text or data revised for correction, consistency, or readability while preserving its underlying content.
- **TRBN — Translated/Transliterated by Najma:** Text translated from one language into another, or Text rendered from one writing system into another.

Multiple labels may apply to the same dataset when different stages of work have been performed. These labels describe editorial contributions; they do not, by themselves, establish textual accuracy, scholarly endorsement, ownership, or licensing rights.

## Sources and Verification

Source identification and verification are important principles of this project.

Each dataset should document, where available:

- The source text, publication, edition, or digital resource.
- The source language and writing system.
- The extraction, conversion, or editorial methods used.
- The scope of manual verification and any known limitations.
- The applicable copyright, license, and redistribution conditions.

A claim of manual verification refers only to the portions and procedures actually checked. Editorial revisions should not be interpreted as proof that every reading is identical to the original printed edition.

## Long-Term Preservation

May’s Corpus is intended to remain organized and documented so that its datasets can be maintained, reviewed, and further developed over time.

The repository is currently maintained as a private project. Any future public release will depend on the applicable rights, licensing conditions, documentation, and readiness of the individual datasets.
